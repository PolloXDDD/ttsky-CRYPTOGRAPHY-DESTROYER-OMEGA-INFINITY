\section{Worst-Case Convergence Analysis: Strict 3-SAT Barrier Family}

The exact SAT encoding and OMEGA Boolean-cell representation do not by
themselves imply polynomial-time global convergence.  For the current
one-cell clause-feedback rule, a strict 3-SAT family gives an explicit
superpolynomial lower bound.

\subsection{Strict 3-CNF Gadgets}

A two-literal clause $D=(a\lor b)$ is replaced by
\begin{equation}
G(D;z)
=
(a\lor b\lor z)
\land
(a\lor b\lor\neg z),
\end{equation}
where $z$ is a fresh variable.  If $D$ is true, both clauses are true; if
$D$ is false, exactly one of the two clauses is false, independently of $z$.

A unit clause $(x)$ is replaced by
\begin{align}
U(x;u,v)={}&
(x\lor u\lor v)
\land(x\lor u\lor\neg v)\\
&\land(x\lor\neg u\lor v)
\land(x\lor\neg u\lor\neg v).
\end{align}
If $x=1$, all four clauses are true.  If $x=0$, exactly one is false,
independently of $u,v$.  All clauses in these gadgets contain three distinct
variables.

\subsection{Barrier Formula}

Let
\begin{equation}
w_i=n-i,\qquad 1\le i\le n-1.
\end{equation}
Start from the weighted equality-chain formula
\begin{equation}
\widetilde F_n
=
(x_n)
\land
\bigwedge_{i=1}^{n-1}
\left[
(\neg x_i\lor x_{i+1})^{w_i}
\land
(x_i\lor\neg x_{i+1})^{w_i}
\right].
\end{equation}
Replace every two-literal copy by its own fresh $G$ gadget and replace the
unit clause by $U$.  Call the resulting strict 3-CNF $F_n$.

The number of strict 3-clauses is
\begin{equation}
m
=
4\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(n-i)+4
=
2n(n-1)+4
=
\Theta(n^2),
\end{equation}
and the number of auxiliary variables is also $\Theta(n^2)$.  The unique
assignment of the original variables satisfying the chain is
\begin{equation}
x^\star=(1,\ldots,1).
\end{equation}

For
\begin{equation}
S_k=0^{\,n-k}1^k,
\end{equation}
the number of violated strict clauses is independent of all gadget auxiliary
bits and equals
\begin{equation}
E(S_0)=1,\qquad
E(S_k)=k\quad(1\le k\le n-1),\qquad
E(S_n)=0.
\end{equation}

At an interior macro-state $S_k$, every violated clause belongs to a boundary
gadget and contains three candidate variables: the zero-side boundary bit,
the one-side boundary bit, and one gadget auxiliary bit.  A forward boundary
flip maps $S_k$ to $S_{k+1}$ and raises the macro-energy by one.  A backward
boundary flip maps $S_k$ to $S_{k-1}$ and lowers it by one for $k\ge2$.
Flipping the gadget auxiliary bit leaves the macro-state and energy unchanged.

Let $p\in(0,1)$ be the exploration probability.  For $k\ge2$,
\begin{equation}
P(S_k\to S_{k+1})=\frac{p}{3},
\end{equation}
whereas
\begin{equation}
P(S_k\to S_{k-1})=1-\frac{2p}{3}.
\end{equation}
At $S_1$, the backward move and the auxiliary move have equal greedy energy,
so the backward-to-forward probability ratio is minimized there.  For every
$1\le k\le n-2$,
\begin{equation}
\frac{P(\mathrm{backward})}{P(\mathrm{forward})}
\ge
R_0
=
\frac{3-p}{2p}
>1.
\end{equation}

Let $K_t$ denote the suffix length, stopping when it first reaches either
$0$ or $L=n-1$.  Since the backward/forward ratio is at least $R_0$,
$R_0^{K_t}$ is a supermartingale.  Optional stopping gives
\begin{equation}
P_1(\tau_L<\tau_0)
\le
\frac{R_0-1}{R_0^L-1}.
\end{equation}
Therefore, beginning from the all-zero macro-state, the expected number of
excursions required merely to reach $S_{n-1}$ obeys
\begin{equation}
\mathbb E[N_{\mathrm{exc}}]
\ge
\frac{R_0^{n-1}-1}{R_0-1}
=
\exp(\Omega(n)).
\end{equation}

For the implementation value $p=0.45$,
\begin{equation}
R_0
=
\frac{3-0.45}{2(0.45)}
=
2.833\ldots.
\end{equation}

If the original Boolean cells are initialized uniformly at random, the event
$S_0=0^n$ has probability $2^{-n}$, regardless of the auxiliary-bit values.
Consequently,
\begin{equation}
\mathbb E[T]
\ge
2^{-n}
\frac{R_0^{n-1}-1}{R_0-1}
=
\Omega\!\left(
\left(\frac{R_0}{2}\right)^n
\right).
\end{equation}
For $p=0.45$,
\begin{equation}
\frac{R_0}{2}=1.4166\ldots>1.
\end{equation}
Thus the expected runtime is exponential in $n$ and superpolynomial in the
strict 3-CNF input size $\Theta(n^2)$.

\begin{theorem}
The current OMEGA greedy/exploratory one-cell clause-feedback dynamics with
$p=0.45$ does not have worst-case polynomial expected global-convergence time
on arbitrary strict 3-SAT inputs.
\end{theorem}

The obstruction is the update rule, not the exact SAT energy: the exact
QUBO/Ising compiler remains correct.  To obtain a valid worst-case
polynomial-time theorem, OMEGA requires a different, genuinely nonlocal
progress mechanism.
